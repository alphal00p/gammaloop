%% Theory cards: the broken phase (the unbroken renormalised action with H -> H_v + phi, linear gauge).

=== card broken-shift
title: The broken phase as a change of variable
report: sec:bl-statement sec:bp-shift sec:bl-fields sec:bp-gauge
---
The broken phase is not renormalised anew. Its action is the renormalised action of the unbroken phase, [[ eqs(Sren, S0 + deltaS1 + DeltaS1ST) ]], written in the variable [[ eqs(phi, H - Hv) ]]: the Higgs doublet is shifted, [[ to(H, Hv + phi) ]], by the constant doublet [[ Hv ]] whose upper component vanishes and whose lower component is [[ v/sqrtn(2) ]], in the same linear gauge and at [[ eqs(xi, 1) ]]. No loop calculation beyond those of the unbroken phase enters.

The report gives three reasons why this is exact. The shift is a change of variable: [[ Hv ]] is constant, so derivatives and functional derivatives with respect to [[ H ]] and [[ phi ]] coincide, and the effective action of the broken phase is that of the unbroken phase at [[ Hv + phi ]], with tadpole graphs part of every Green's function. The counterterms are local polynomials in [[ H ]]: the poles and the finite breaking [[ X1 ]] are fixed by their Taylor coefficients at [[ eqs(H, 0) ]], and the Taylor operator counts every mass and every vertex proportional to [[ v ]] as a small quantity, so the coefficients computed with massless propagators are the complete one-loop input. The gauge fixing does not depend on [[ H ]]: the Slavnov–Taylor operator and the linear identities keep their form, the breaking [[ Delta1 ]] does not involve [[ H ]], and the BRST transformation of the fluctuation is [[ eqs(ord(sop, phi), i*ord(wm, par(Hv + phi))) ]]. Rigid SU(2)×U(1) invariance stays exact, realised non-linearly on [[ phi ]].

The vacuum value [[ v ]] is not a parameter of the action: it is defined by the minimum of the tree-level potential written with the MS-bar parameters, [[ eqs(m2, -lambda*v^2) ]] with [[ m2 ]] negative ({card:broken-tadpole|tadpoles}). No input from an [[ field(R, xi) ]] gauge is used. That gauge depends on [[ Hv ]] and is a different action: it breaks rigid invariance unless [[ Hv ]] is replaced by an external doublet, and it needs Green's functions that the report lists but does not compute ({sec:bp-gauge}).

=== card broken-masses
title: Masses and mass counterterms in the broken phase
report: sec:bl-fields sec:bl-tree sec:bl-dS sec:bp-fermion
---
The tree-level masses are [[ eqs(M_W, g*v/2) ]], [[ eqs(M_Z, M_W/c_W) ]] with [[ eqs(c_W, g/sqrtn(g^2 + gp^2)) ]], [[ eqs(M_h^2, 2*lambda*v^2) ]] and [[ eqs(field(m, k), y_k*v/sqrtn(2)) ]] for k = u, d, e; the photon, the gluons, the neutrino, the Goldstone fields [[ G0 ]], [[ Gp ]] and all ghosts are massless in this gauge. They are MS-bar masses at the tree-level minimum, not pole masses. The massive Dirac fields are built from physical components, for example [[ eqs(uf, ord(PL, Psi(Q, up(1))) + ord(PR, U)) ]], and their sterile partners from the [[ chi(f) ]], [[ eqs(ufprime, ord(PR, Psi(Q, up(1))) + ord(PL, U)) ]], where [[ Psi(Q, up(1)) ]] is the upper SU(2) component of the quark doublet.

The sterile partners change the fermion propagator. The mass term couples only the physical components, while the d-dimensional kinetic term couples [[ uf ]] and [[ ufprime ]] through [[ slashhat(p) ]]. The propagator of the pair has the denominator [[ par(sq(p))^2 - m_u^2*sqbar(p) ]], which is not d-dimensionally covariant; its physical block reduces to the standard [[ i*(slashbar(p) + m_u)/(sqbar(p) - m_u^2) ]] at vanishing hatted momentum, where external fermions live. The R-operation is unchanged: the mass can be treated as a two-point insertion on massless propagators, so poles and the finite breaking are polynomials in the masses. At one loop the finite parts can be traded for those of the standard propagator plus a local term ({sec:bp-fermion}); beyond one loop this is not derived.

The mass counterterms follow from the unbroken-phase constants by the shift. In units of [[ lfe ]], [[ dpar(M_W^2)/M_W^2 ]] is [[ -13/3*g^2 + 1/2*gp^2 - Ytil ]], with [[ eqs(Ytil, 3*y_u^2 + 3*y_d^2 + y_e^2) ]]; the bare-mass shift of the up quark, [[ eqs(dpar(m_u), m_u*(dpar(y_u)/y_u + 1/2*dZ(H))) ]], is [[ m_u*(3/4*(y_u^2 - y_d^2) - 4*g_s^2 - 3/8*g^2 - 11/24*gp^2) ]]; and [[ eqs(dpar(M_h^2), M_h^2*(dpar(lambda)/lambda + dZ(H))) ]]. Under the normalisation (N1) there is no finite counterterm for the fermion masses, the Yukawa vertices or the scalar potential; the gauge bosons receive finite mass-like terms ({card:broken-st|finite terms after the shift}). Not computed: broken-phase finite parts at non-zero external momenta.

=== card broken-mixing
title: Gauge–Goldstone and neutral gauge mixing
report: sec:bl-tree sec:bl-dS sec:bl-ST sec:bp-gauge
---
The neutral gauge fields are rotated with the weak mixing angle, [[ eqs(Z(mu), c_W*W(mu, 3) - s_W*B(mu)) ]] and [[ eqs(Agam(mu), s_W*W(mu, 3) + c_W*B(mu)) ]], with [[ eqs(e, g*s_W) ]]; the same rotation is applied to the ghosts, antighosts, auxiliary fields and sources. Because the gauge fixing is linear and does not depend on [[ H ]], the tree-level mixing between gauge and Goldstone fields stays in the quadratic action,

[[ M_Z*ord(Z(mu), pd(up(mu), G0)) + i*M_W*(ord(Wm(mu), pd(up(mu), Gp)) - ord(Wp(mu), pd(up(mu), Gm))) ]]

and enters the propagators: there are mixed gauge–Goldstone propagators and double poles at [[ eqs(sq(k), 0) ]] in the longitudinal and Goldstone parts. This is a consistent gauge, in which the broken-phase action is complete. The [[ field(R, xi) ]] gauge would cancel this mixing but is a different action ({card:broken-shift|the shift}).

The singular counterterm mixes the neutral fields. The field constants of [[ Z(mu) ]] and [[ Agam(mu) ]] form a matrix, the rotation of the diagonal matrix of [[ dZ(W) ]] and [[ dZ(B) ]]; in units of [[ lfe ]] its entries are [[ (33*g^2*c_W^2 - 43*gp^2*s_W^2)/18 ]] for Z Z, [[ -5/9*e^2 ]] for the photon and [[ s_W*c_W*(33*g^2 + 43*gp^2)/18 ]] for the Z–photon element. In the same units the mass counterterm contains the Z–photon term [[ -2*g^2*s_W*c_W*M_Z^2*ord(Z(mu), Agam(up(mu))) ]] and no photon mass term, and the mixing counterterm contains a new term [[ -2*g^2*s_W*c_W*M_Z*ord(Agam(mu), pd(up(mu), G0)) ]]. The Yukawa parts of these counterterms multiply barred components only, since they come from fermion loops.

The finite restoring counterterm changes the mixing by [[ -Ytil/3 ]] times the tree-level mixing above (times [[ lf ]]), with [[ eqs(Ytil, 3*y_u^2 + 3*y_d^2 + y_e^2) ]]; it comes from the current monomials of the unbroken phase. There is no finite photon–Goldstone term, because the two current coefficients are equal, and no finite gauge–Higgs term. The longitudinal and mixing terms change only unphysical parts of the two-point functions, but they enter every loop calculation in this gauge.

=== card broken-tadpole
title: Tadpoles and the vacuum value
report: sec:bl-fields sec:bl-dS sec:bp-tadpole sec:bp-needed
---
The vacuum value [[ v ]] is defined by the minimum of the tree-level potential written with the MS-bar renormalised parameters, [[ eqs(m2, -lambda*v^2) ]] with [[ m2 ]] negative. It is a function of [[ m2 ]] and [[ lambda ]], not a parameter of the action, and it is not renormalised. With this convention the tree-level tadpole term [[ -v*(m2 + lambda*v^2)*h ]] vanishes, while the tadpole graphs are kept in every Green's function; this is the treatment of Fleischer and Jegerlehner.

The singular counterterm then contains a tadpole term [[ -v*dpar(T)*h ]] with

[[ eqs(dpar(T), dpar(m2) + v^2*dpar(lambda) + lambda*v^2*dZ(H)) ]]

which equals [[ lfe*v^2*(6*lambda^2 - lambda*Cinv(H) - field(Y, 4) + 3/16*field(G, 4)) ]], with [[ eqs(Cinv(H), 3/4*g^2 + 1/4*gp^2) ]], [[ eqs(field(Y, 4), 3*y_u^4 + 3*y_d^4 + y_e^4) ]] and [[ eqs(field(G, 4), 3*g^4 + 2*g^2*gp^2 + gp^4) ]]. It cancels the pole of the one-loop tadpole; it contains the field-renormalisation part [[ lambda*v^2*dZ(H) ]] besides [[ dpar(m2) + v^2*dpar(lambda) ]]. The Goldstone mass counterterm equals [[ dpar(T) ]], as required by the relation between the Goldstone two-point function at zero momentum and the tadpole. Only the [[ lambda^2 ]] part of the tadpole pole was checked by hand; the finite part of the one-loop tadpole is not computed in the report. The finite restoring counterterm gives no tadpole and no scalar two-point term.

Other conventions differ by a finite reparametrisation of [[ v ]]. If the renormalised tadpole is set to zero, [[ v ]] is the minimum of the effective potential, which depends on the gauge, and the MS-bar masses depend on [[ xi ]]. With a non-anticommuting [[ gamma5 ]] the gauge independence of the convention used here is expected to need the restored identity and a control of the [[ xi ]] dependence; this is not derived, and the report works at [[ eqs(xi, 1) ]] only.

=== card broken-st
title: Finite restoring counterterms after the shift
report: sec:bl-ST sec:bl-massless sec:bp-cms sec:bl-implications
---
The finite counterterm of the broken phase is [[ DeltaS1ST ]] evaluated at [[ eqs(H, Hv + phi) ]]; no coefficient is recomputed. Its [[ H ]]-dependent coefficients are purely Yukawa, so after the shift the terms with [[ Hv ]] are proportional to fermion masses squared. They give the gauge bosons finite mass-like terms (times [[ lf ]]):

[[ -g^2/2*(3*m_u^2 + 3*m_d^2 + m_e^2)*ord(Wp(mu), Wm(up(mu))) - 1/2*g_s^2*(m_u^2 + m_d^2)*ord(G(mu, A), G(up(mu), A)) - e^2*(4/3*m_u^2 + 1/3*m_d^2 + m_e^2)*ord(Agam(mu), Agam(up(mu))) ]]

and Z–Z and Z–photon terms. For two neutral bosons the term has the form of a mass term, one half of [[ lf ]] times the two fields times [[ -2*sumover(f, Nc(f)*m_f^2*(field(v, f)*prime(field(v, f)) + 3*field(a, f)*prime(field(a, f)))) ]], with [[ field(v, f) ]], [[ field(a, f) ]] the vector and axial couplings of the fermion f to the first boson, primed for the second, and [[ Nc(f) ]] its colour multiplicity. Under the shift the current terms change the gauge–Goldstone mixing by [[ -Ytil/3 ]] times its tree value, and the [[ h ]]-linear terms are [[ 2*h/v ]] times the mass-like ones.

The photon and gluon mass terms look alarming but are required. The report computes the fermion-loop two-point functions of the photon, of Z–photon and of the gluon at zero momentum, to order [[ m_f^2 ]], with complete finite parts. The finite local part per fermion is [[ 2*field(v, f)*prime(field(v, f)) + 26/3*field(a, f)*prime(field(a, f)) ]]; of it, [[ -2*field(v, f)*prime(field(v, f)) + 14/3*field(a, f)*prime(field(a, f)) ]] comes from the difference between the propagator with sterile partners and the standard one. The counterterm contributes [[ -2*(field(v, f)*prime(field(v, f)) + 3*field(a, f)*prime(field(a, f))) ]], and the sum, [[ 8/3*field(a, f)*prime(field(a, f)) ]], vanishes for the photon, Z–photon and gluon cases, where one of the couplings is vectorial. The photon, Z–photon and gluon two-point functions at zero momentum therefore stay massless; an independent evaluation through the Slavnov–Taylor identity of the ghost–photon legs gives the same constant ({sec:bl-massless}).

The longitudinal terms, for example [[ 2/3*g^2*ord(pd(mu, Wp(up(mu))), pd(nu, Wm(up(nu)))) ]] and [[ 8/9*e^2*par(pd(mu, Agam(up(mu))))^2 ]], and the mixing change only unphysical parts. The W and Z poles depend on the mass-like coefficients and therefore on the restoring counterterm. The remaining 33 non-zero coefficients multiply monomials without [[ H ]] and are untouched by the shift.

=== card broken-interactions
title: Interaction terms generated by the shift
report: sec:bl-tree sec:bl-dS sec:bl-ST
---
Every interaction of the broken phase descends from an operator of the unbroken phase under [[ to(H, Hv + phi) ]], with [[ phi ]] containing the Higgs boson [[ h ]] and the Goldstone fields [[ G0 ]], [[ Gp ]], [[ Gm ]]. The scalar kinetic term gives the couplings of [[ h ]] to [[ ord(Wp, Wm) ]] and [[ ord(Z, Z) ]], for instance [[ 2*h/v*(M_W^2*ord(Wp(mu), Wm(up(mu))) + 1/2*M_Z^2*ord(Z(mu), Z(up(mu)))) ]], and Goldstone–gauge–gauge vertices; the potential gives cubic and quartic scalar couplings proportional to [[ lambda*v ]] and [[ lambda ]]; the Yukawa terms give [[ -m_f*par(1 + h/v)*ord(bar(f), f) ]] together with [[ G0 ]] couplings with [[ gamma5 ]] and [[ Gp ]] couplings with chiral projectors, all with physical components only. In the fermion currents the charged fields couple with [[ g/sqrtn(2) ]] times the raising and lowering combinations of Pauli matrices, the photon with [[ e*q ]] and the Z with [[ g/c_W*(field(T, up(3)) - s_W^2*q) ]], where q is the charge matrix and [[ field(T, up(3)) ]] the third isospin generator. Ghosts couple only to gauge fields, so there is no ghost–scalar vertex in this gauge; the Higgs source term gives a source–ghost two-point vertex proportional to [[ M_W ]] and [[ M_Z ]].

The singular counterterms of these vertices are those of the unbroken phase at [[ Hv + phi ]]. The quartic scalar coupling carries [[ dpar(lambda) + 2*lambda*dZ(H) ]], equal to [[ lfe*(12*lambda^2 - 2*lambda*Cinv(H) - field(Y, 4) + 3/16*field(G, 4)) ]], and so do the cubic couplings proportional to [[ v ]]; every Yukawa-derived term of fermion k, mass term included, carries the factor [[ field(c, k) ]] of the Yukawa vertex; the fermion currents carry [[ -lfe*(Cinv(f) + Cad(r) + Ycal(f)) ]] for the factor r; the cubic and quartic gauge self-couplings keep their unbroken-phase factors.

The finite restoring counterterm adds, besides the mass-like terms, the [[ h ]]-linear and [[ h^2 ]] terms ([[ 2*h/v ]] and [[ h^2/v^2 ]] times the mass-like ones), [[ -Ytil/3 ]] times the tree scalar–scalar–gauge vertices, the vertex [[ -(y_u^2 + 2*y_d^2)*M_W*ord(par(e*Agam(mu) - gp*s_W*Z(mu)), par(ord(Gp, Wm(up(mu))) + ord(Gm, Wp(up(mu))))) ]], further scalar–scalar–gauge–gauge terms, and the fermion–gauge vertices and source terms of the unbroken phase rewritten in the mass basis. In each Exposé of the broken phase the graphs shown are those of the parent operators of the unbroken phase.
