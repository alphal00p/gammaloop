=== text title
---
One-loop renormalisation of the Standard Model with a non-anticommuting γ₅

=== text subtitle
---
The renormalised Lagrangian operator by operator: one fermion family, BMHV scheme with sterile partners, MS-bar, finite Slavnov–Taylor-restoring counterterms, Feynman gauge, unbroken and broken phase.

=== text status
---
This page presents the complete one-loop renormalised action [[ eqs(Sren, S0 + deltaS1 + DeltaS1ST) ]] of the Standard Model with one fermion family, regularised in [[ eqs(d, 4 - 2*ep) ]] dimensions with the BMHV treatment of [[ gamma5 ]]: for every operator its coefficient in the classical action, in the singular counterterm and in the finite restoring counterterm, its Feynman rules, the one-loop graphs that determine it and the theory behind it. It is a picture of what first-class Lagrangian support in HepKIT and a multi-loop BMHV automation are meant to produce directly; today it is assembled from the sources listed below.

<strong>Generated by the automation.</strong> The Python package <code>hepkit_renorm</code> of this repository starts from the model specification of the one-family Standard Model (gauge group, chiral multiplets, scalar doublet, classical four-dimensional Lagrangian, linear gauge fixing at [[ eqs(xi, 1) ]]) and derives everything else: the sterile partners, ghosts and BRST sources, the regularised action and the breaking [[ Delta1 ]], the classes of Green's functions, the operator bases, the one-loop graphs (enumerated by the package and cross-checked against the HepKIT diagram generator) and their pole and evanescent finite parts, the singular counterterm, the finite breaking [[ X1 ]] and the restoring counterterm under the report's normalisation conditions. On this run the twenty renormalisation constants, the eight beta functions, the 71 non-zero components of [[ X1 ]] and all 97 restoring coefficients (39 of them non-zero) agree with the report, and all fifteen checks of the run pass. The graphs drawn on this site are the graphs of that run, rendered with HepKIT at the level of multiplets.

<strong>Taken from the report.</strong> The theory texts, the names of the basis monomials, the section and page references and the cross-checks quoted in the texts are taken from the report <a data-report-page="1">One-loop renormalisation of the Standard Model with a non-anticommuting γ₅</a>. The package has no broken-phase representation; the operators of the broken phase are derived on this site from those of the unbroken phase by the shift of the Higgs doublet and the rotation to the mass basis of the report, and are checked against the automation's order store and against the report's broken-phase formulas.

<strong>Not covered.</strong> Two-loop quantities (the construction beyond one loop is argued in the report and tested on a toy graph only); the dependence on the gauge parameter (all results hold in the Feynman gauge); the R<sub>ξ</sub> gauge and the complex-mass scheme, which the report describes but does not compute; broken-phase loop functions other than the zero-momentum check of the massless gauge bosons, in particular the finite tadpole and the two-point functions at non-zero momentum; several fermion families and flavour mixing.

=== text intro-unbroken
---
Each row of the table is one operator of the renormalised action [[ eqs(Sren, S0 + deltaS1 + DeltaS1ST) ]] around [[ eqs(H, 0) ]], with its coefficients, Feynman rules and the graphs that fix them.

The unbroken phase is the expansion around [[ eqs(H, 0) ]], with the mass parameter [[ m2 ]] of either sign. Each row of the table is one operator of the renormalised action [[ eqs(Sren, S0 + deltaS1 + DeltaS1ST) ]] with definite field content: [[ S0 ]] is the regularised classical action in [[ eqs(d, 4 - 2*ep) ]] dimensions (gauge fixing in the Feynman gauge, ghosts, BRST sources and the sterile partners of the fermions included), [[ deltaS1 ]] the singular MS-bar counterterm, whose coefficients contain [[ lfe ]] and which includes evanescent operators, and [[ DeltaS1ST ]] the finite counterterm that restores the Slavnov–Taylor identity, whose coefficients contain [[ lf ]]. The breaking [[ eqs(Delta1, ord(sop, S0)) ]] is not part of the action and is listed for its Feynman rule. Every Feynman rule is [[ i ]] times the vertex function [[ field(V) ]] shown, with all momenta incoming.

The operators, coefficients and vertex functions are those of the automation run; the Exposé of an operator shows the one-loop graphs of the Green's functions that fix its coefficients and a card of theory taken from the report.

=== text intro-broken
---
Each row of the table is one operator of the renormalised action after the shift [[ to(H, Hv + phi) ]], in the mass basis, with the operators of the unbroken phase from which it descends.

The broken phase is not renormalised separately. Its action is the renormalised action of the unbroken phase with the Higgs doublet shifted, [[ to(H, Hv + phi) ]], where [[ Hv ]] is the constant doublet with lower component [[ v/sqrtn(2) ]], in the same linear gauge at [[ eqs(xi, 1) ]]; no input from an [[ field(R, xi) ]] gauge is used. The vacuum value [[ v ]] is defined at the tree-level minimum of the potential written with the MS-bar parameters, [[ eqs(m2, -lambda*v^2) ]]; tadpole graphs are kept, and the singular counterterm contains a tadpole term. The masses [[ M_W ]], [[ M_Z ]], [[ M_h ]] and [[ field(m, k) ]] are tree-level MS-bar masses, not pole masses.

No loop calculation beyond those of the unbroken phase enters: every coefficient below descends from an operator of the unbroken phase, and the Exposé of a term shows the graphs of its parent operators. The fields are the mass eigenstates; the massive fermions have sterile partners, which change their propagator but not the counterterms. Apart from a hand check of the [[ lambda^2 ]] part of the tadpole pole, the only broken-phase loop calculation of the report is that of the fermion-loop two-point functions of the photon, of Z–photon mixing and of the gluon at zero momentum; it confirms that these bosons stay massless once the finite restoring terms are included.

%% Definitions of the five types of terms and the conventions of the home page (manifest "types", "conventions").

=== text type-tree
---
Classical action [[ S0 ]], including gauge fixing, ghosts and the BRST source terms.

=== text type-pole
---
Singular counterterm [[ deltaS1 ]] on a non-evanescent operator, continued to [[ d ]] dimensions as in {sec:evanescent|Section 3.8.6}. Its coefficient includes [[ lfe ]].

=== text type-evanescent
---
Singular counterterm on an evanescent operator, one that vanishes in four dimensions. Its coefficient includes [[ lfe ]].

=== text type-finite
---
Finite symmetry-restoring counterterm [[ eqs(DeltaS1ST, lf*sumover(alpha, rc(alpha)*Qop(alpha))) ]], which restores the Slavnov–Taylor identity.

=== text type-breaking
---
Insertion [[ eqs(Delta1, ord(sop, S0)) ]] of the tree-level breaking. It is not part of the action; it is listed for its Feynman rule.

=== text conventions
---
<ul><li>Dimensional regularisation with [[ eqs(d, 4 - 2*ep) ]] (the lunate [[ ep ]]); the Levi-Civita symbol is the curly [[ levi(mu, nu, rho, sigma) ]].</li><li>Barred objects are four-dimensional, hatted objects [[ (d - 4) ]]-dimensional.</li><li>A vertex function [[ field(V) ]] is listed for each operator; the Feynman rule is [[ i ]] times [[ field(V) ]]. All momenta are incoming unless a leg label says otherwise.</li><li>Renormalisation constants such as [[ dZ(H) ]] include the factor [[ lfe ]]; the finite counterterm is [[ eqs(DeltaS1ST, lf*sumover(alpha, rc(alpha)*Qop(alpha))) ]].</li><li>Feynman gauge [[ eqs(xi, 1) ]], one fermion family, MS-bar. The broken-phase Lagrangian follows from the shift [[ to(H, Hv + phi) ]].</li></ul>
