## Precision triage and directive diagnosis

On the restored original basis, the correlated `e13` path has `p=0.999824..1.000461` across both branches and 8/12/16-point windows, with minimum R² `0.999999787`. Its active threshold contribution is therefore integrable; the old f64 `p≈4` tail was cancellation loss, not evidence for a projected CT.

The prior rank-two `e12/e13` f64 failure is likewise a bounded Arb plateau: `p=-0.000605..0.000594`. Its low R² (`0.4975..0.7613`) is the expected consequence of fitting an almost constant series and is not growth. Across all 32 signed points its magnitude changes by only a factor `1.01834`. The other process-valid hard-collinear family, `e13/e14`, is even flatter: `p=-0.00007113..0.00007199` and a maximum/minimum magnitude ratio `1.00218`. These are finite limits, not divergent fits.
