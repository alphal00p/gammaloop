## Precision triage and directive diagnosis

The combined correlated `e13` path is the only threshold-soft result selected for Arb: its f64 failure is smooth, branch-consistent, correctly scaled, and its threshold component alone has `p=3.99083..4.00595` with minimum R2 `.999915`. The original contribution is noisy (`p=3.419..5.174`, minimum R2 `.906392`). The exact 50-point, all-orientation Arb block ran for 332.335 seconds including cleanup but did not finish before its 300-second evaluation guard; no partial approach JSON was emitted.

Under the lease precision rule, an uncompleted Arb check cannot confirm a physical failure or justify a directive. The clean DOT therefore remains unmodified, threshold subtraction remains enabled, and no threshold-only path is applicable. The f64 fail is retained rather than hidden, but no smallest projected subspace is inferred from insufficient precision.
