### Why the remaining E22 obstruction is physical and unavoidable

At q14=0, the E20, E42, and E43 surfaces coalesce on

```text
sqrt(MT^2+|K1|^2) + sqrt(MT^2+|K2|^2) + |K1-K2| = 1000.
```

E22 is the two-top surface
`2*sqrt(MT^2+|K1|^2)-1000`. It cannot be made exterior at this intersection:
if its first energy exceeded 500, the massive triangle inequality

```text
sqrt(MT^2+|K2|^2) + |K1-K2|
  >= sqrt(MT^2+|K1|^2)
```

would make the coalesced E20/E42/E43 sum exceed 1000. Thus E22 is necessarily
active and cannot legitimately be disabled or avoided by choosing a more
generic hard complement. Its common boundary with the other surfaces occurs
only with the additional q12-soft equality. This explains why freezing q12 in
a one-loop E22 solve has no common center, while enlarging the solve to two or
three loops reaches a nested boundary whose radial solution the current
projected construction still rejects.

Disabling E22 would make the diagnostic run, but would remove a genuine
physical two-top threshold and violate the requirement to retain proper
threshold subtraction. Exact-zero or epsilon multipliers are likewise not a
valid remedy: the root is constructed before a generic nonzero multiplier, and
an epsilon gate would change the integrand. The strongest complementary E26
partition consequently cannot be retained even though it cures all three
generic soft powers.

### Conclusion

GL098 is restored pristine. The remediation identified the required structural
feature—a one-/two-loop separation is needed for the E26 soft cancellation—but
the retained E22 physical overlap cannot be represented by a projected radial
CT that is valid on every required path. Every available parent, subspace
dimension, precision lane, and topology-valid generation basis either leaves
that root invalid or introduces a formal soft divergence. The graph therefore
remains **unresolved (`all limits OK = no`)**, with the exact no-go evidence
preserved under `states/GL098_guard`. The selected final-state UV sectors all
pass; that UV result does not repair the independent E22 threshold-root
obstruction or upgrade the local-UV fallback to full integrated UV.
