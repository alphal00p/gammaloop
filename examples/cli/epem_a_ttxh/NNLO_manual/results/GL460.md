## Topology and threshold inventory

The loop-momentum basis is `(e2,e4,e7,e11)=(K0,K1,K2,K3)`.  The relevant
massless momenta are

```text
q13 = K1-K2,
q14 = K3-K2,
```

so the two independent soft constraints have rank three each and their common
locus has rank six.  GL460 has no `V_36` or `V_37`, hence no mandatory
hard-collinear sector.  The active threshold associations are

| cut | cut edges | active left E-surfaces |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | `(2,5,8,13)` | E36, E8 |
| 1 | `(2,4,5)` | E8, E28, E35, E44, E46, E38* |
| 2 | `(2,3,12,14)` | E36, E8, E28 |

| 4 | `(2,3,7,13)` | none |
| 5 | `(2,3,6)` | E8, E28, E26, E27* |

For `Et(k)=sqrt(|k|^2+173^2)`, their distinct equations are

```text
eta8          = Et(K2)+Et(K3)+|q14|-1000
eta26 = eta46 = Et(K1)+Et(K2)+|q13|-1000
eta27 = eta38 = Et(K1)+Et(K2)+Et(K3)+Et(K1+K3-K2)-1000
eta28         = 2 Et(K2)-1000
eta35         = Et(K1)+Et(K1+K3-K2)+|q14|-1000
eta36         = Et(K2)+Et(K1+K3-K2)+|q13|+|q14|-1000
eta44         = Et(K1)+Et(K3)+|q13|+|q14|-1000.
```

At `q14=0`, cut-1 E35 becomes the singleton equation
`2 Et(K1)-1000`.  The other cut-1 families reduce to E8/E28,
E44/E46, and singleton E38; consequently E35 cannot be silently dropped or
merged with an active same-cut sibling.
