## Routing and singular loci

GL460 has four loops and no `V_36` or `V_37`, hence no applicable
hard-collinear sector. The raw spatial routing is

```text

e3  = K1-K0              t
e4  = K1                 t, lmb_id 1
e5  = K1-K0              t
e6  = K1                 t
e7  = K2                 t, lmb_id 2
e8  = K2                 t
e11 = K3                 t, lmb_id 3
e12 = K1+K3-K2           t
e13 = K1-K2              g
e14 = K3-K2              g
```

Thus `q13=K1-K2` and `q14=K3-K2` are independent rank-3 soft
constraints; their common locus has rank 6. For
`Et(k)=sqrt(|k|^2+173^2)`, the distinct active equations are

```text
eta8          = Et(K2)+Et(K3)+|q14|-1000
eta26 = eta46 = Et(K1)+Et(K2)+|q13|-1000
eta27 = eta38 = Et(K1)+Et(K2)+Et(K3)+Et(K1+K3-K2)-1000
eta28         = 2 Et(K2)-1000
eta35         = Et(K1)+Et(K1+K3-K2)+|q14|-1000
eta36         = Et(K2)+Et(K1+K3-K2)+|q13|+|q14|-1000
eta44         = Et(K1)+Et(K3)+|q13|+|q14|-1000.
```

At q13 soft the coalescing families are E26/E46/E28,
E8/E35/E36/E44, and the starred E27/E38 family. At q14 soft they are
E26/E46/E36/E44, E8/E28, E35, and E27/E38. At double soft all nonstar
instances share `2 Et(K2)-1000`, while E27/E38 share

## Directive decision

No `threshold_counterterms` directive is accepted. All q13 families and two
q14 families pass, but five mandatory q14/double paths are unevaluable and the
nonstar double family has poor, inconsistent fits. The available evidence does
not uniquely identify a safe projected association. Threshold subtraction
remains enabled. Since the DOT was not edited, no post-edit threshold-only
path is required.

## Conclusion

`threshold struct. edited? = no`; `converged? = yes`; `all limits OK? =
inconclusive`. GL460 has clean, strong q13 cancellation evidence and two
passing q14 correlated families, but generic q14 and several correlated/double
families hit a threshold-driven CT radial-root blocker. With no smooth active
fit and no evidence-unique projected parent, no Arb run or directive is
accepted; the unresolved paths and threshold-off diagnostic remain fully
reproducible in the card and state.
