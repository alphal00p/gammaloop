## Directive and precision diagnosis

Threshold subtraction is active in the final evidence: `threshold_counterterm_0` is nonzero at all 100 points of every threshold-on series, reaching `2.65e-34` on the common double-soft path. On the exact same common path, threshold-off produces essentially identical powers, fit quality, and branch disagreement. Thus the rank-6 behavior is consistent with generic f64 cancellation noise rather than demonstrated threshold-counterterm damage.

The GL297 common minimal projection would be the preferred cure if a smooth threshold-driven failure appeared at this four-surface common zero. It is not justified by these data. No GL638 eta-squared partition, epsilon regulator, symmetrization, or late exact-zero gate is introduced. Since no association was edited, an edited-association threshold-only verification is not applicable. The Arb gate is also absent because threshold-off provides no material restoration.
