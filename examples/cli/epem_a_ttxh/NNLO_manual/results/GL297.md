## Threshold diagnosis and directive changes

The original compact cure forced `(5,7)` and `(5,11,13)` under cut `(2,4,9)` into `[5]`, and `(3,9)` and `(3,11,12)` under cut `(2,6,7)` into `[3]`. With every orientation present, the registry additionally materialized:

- `(5,9,12,13)` under `(2,4,9)` in legacy two-loop subspace `[5,11]`;
- `(3,7,12,13)` under `(2,6,7)` in legacy two-loop subspace `[3,11]`.

Writing `p3=K0+K1-K2`, `q12=p3-K3`, and `q13=K3-K1`, both bridge tuples have the same threshold equation

```text
E(K1) + E(p3) + |q12| + |q13| - 1000 = 0.
```

It coalesces with `(5,11,13)` when `q12` is soft, with `(3,11,12)` when `q13` is soft, and with all four ordinary threshold geometries in the double-soft limit. The baseline all-orientation `q12` correlated run had a smooth `p = 3.00001 ... 3.00027`; the baseline `q13` run failed finalization on an invalid projected radial solution. The smallest matching projections were therefore added:

```toml
cut (2,4,9), threshold (5,9,12,13): subspace [5]
cut (2,6,7), threshold (3,7,12,13): subspace [3]
```

After clean full-UV regeneration all 18 threshold associations resolve to one-loop subspaces. The `q13` radial-root failure disappears. The corrected totals exhibit cancellations several orders beyond their separate original/CT terms, but those residuals enter f64 precision noise at the closest scales. A guarded all-orientation arbitrary-precision `q12` attempt ran for 240 seconds and was cleanly terminated without producing a partial JSON; it was not repeated.

This is an unresolved blocker associated with the newly active all-orientation E62 bridge, not a threshold-off workaround. The integration record is correctly marked missing/non-converged.

## Conclusion

All-orientation generation revealed two bridge counterterms hidden by the earlier selected-orientation audit. Projecting them into the neighboring one-loop `[5]` and `[3]` frames removes the observed `q13` radial-root failure and produces strong LU cancellations, while keeping threshold subtraction active. The required f64 asymptotic evidence is nevertheless precision-limited, and the E62 projected overlap center blocks integration. GL297 is therefore **unresolved/inconclusive**, with a complete reproducible card and preserved diagnostics rather than a claimed cure.
