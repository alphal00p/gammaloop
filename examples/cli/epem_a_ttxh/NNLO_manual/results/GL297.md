## Why no GL638-style duplicate was accepted

A smooth partition of unity can distribute a valid subtraction between equivalent projections, but each generated duplicate is still evaluated. Here the rejected E62 projections fail center/root construction before an ordinary smooth multiplier can suppress the bad value. Unless the multiplier is exactly zero on the failing branch, a GL638-style duplicate does not remove the invalid evaluation. The resource strategy's piecewise/step-function selection is not presently represented by the tested directive machinery, so inventing a regulator, disabling E62, or accepting an uncancelled `p≈3` candidate would not be a valid cure.
