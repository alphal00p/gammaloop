## Topology and routing

GL590 has four loops and two internal gluons, e13/e14. It contains neither
`V_36` nor `V_37`, so no hard gluon-collinear sector is required. The clean DOT
preserves basis edges e3/e4/e6/e7 and contains none of `pin`, `dir`, `lmb_rep`,
or `threshold_counterterms`.

Writing `(K0,K1,K2,K3)=(a,b,c,d)` and suppressing the incoming spatial sum,

```text
q3=a, q4=q12=b, q5=q10=a+c-b, q6=c, q7=q8=d,
q13=b-d  (g), q14=c-b  (g).
```

Thus e13 soft is `d=b`, e14 soft is `c=b`, and independent double soft is
`c=d=b`; the corresponding IR ranks are 3, 3, and 6. With `Q=a+c-b`, the
active eta equations used for target validation are

```text
E3  = 2 Et(Q)-1000
E4  = Et(a)+Et(Q)+|c-b|-1000
E19 = Et(b)+Et(c)+|c-b|-1000
E20 = Et(c)+Et(d)+|b-d|+|c-b|-1000
E28 = 2 Et(d)-1000
E41 = 2 Et(b)-1000
E42 = E45 = Et(b)+Et(d)+|b-d|-1000,
Et(k)=sqrt(|k|^2+173^2).
```

At e13 soft the threshold families are E28/E41/E42/E45, E19/E20,
standalone E3, and standalone E4. At e14 soft they are E3/E4, E19/E41,
standalone E28, and E20/E42/E45. At double soft E19/E20/E28/E41/E42/E45
coalesce, while E3/E4 form the other family; the final path activates both.
