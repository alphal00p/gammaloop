sector. With zero incoming spatial momentum sum, the raw routing is

```text

e3  = K1-K0           t
e4  = K1              t
e5  = K1-K0           t
e6  = K1              t, lmb_id 1
```

constraints; their common locus `K3=0, K1=K2` has rank 6. E20/E43,
E22/E35, and E31/E39 are distinct E-surface instances with identical
kinematic equations before any limit.

At q11 soft the groups are E22/E35/E23/E34 and E20/E43/E31/E39. At q14
soft they are E22/E35/E31/E39 and E20/E43/E34. At double soft all eight
active instances coalesce into one radial equation.
