GL148 has four loops and LMB basis edges `(2,4,6,12)`. Vertex 7 is `V_36`, incident on the three gluons `e12`, `e13`, and `e14`; no `V_37` occurs. The raw diagnostic momentum decomposition gives, for spatial loop momenta,

```text
q12 = K3
q13 = K1 - K2 - K3
q14 = K2 - K1
q12 + q13 + q14 = 0.
```

Consequently the independent IR families are three single-soft loci, one rank-six double-soft locus (any two soft gluons force the third soft), and three possible rank-two hard-collinear energy-flow sectors.
