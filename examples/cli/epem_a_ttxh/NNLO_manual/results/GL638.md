# GL638 manual IR-safe threshold audit

## Outcome

- **Threshold structure edited:** yes — the normalized standalone graph is dressed with the established GL638 projected-variant prescription. No exploratory change made during this lease was accepted into the final DOT.
- **All limits OK:** **inconclusive**. The first threshold-on, all-orientation `q13` soft evaluation aborts before producing samples because a local multiplier is `NaN+NaNi`; all remaining required limits are present as explicit card blocks but were not run after this decisive blocker.
- **UV mode:** full local and integrated UV. The current release completed direct-import integrated-UV generation; the local-only fallback was not used.
- **Integration converged under the campaign's rough rule:** yes. The final completed checkpoint is finite, has no nonfinite samples, and has componentwise-absolute relative errors below 75%. It nevertheless contains 0.0305% retained unstable evaluations and cannot validate the unresolved explicit limit.
- **Scientific conclusion:** the selected-orientation GL638 cure is not validated for the unrestricted orientation sum. Its complementary multiplier has an all-orientation `0/0` locus, and no accepted IR-safe replacement was established during this lease.

## Final threshold-counterterm structure

The final DOT deliberately retains the established prescription rather than committing an unvalidated regulator:

- For `(7,8)` under `c0`, `c1`, and `c3`, two projected E-surface instances share parent LMB `[3,4,7,10]`:
  - `intrinsic_1l`, subspace `[7]`, weighted by `eta(star,eset(7,8))^2 / (eta(star,eset(7,8))^2 + eta(star,eset(8,12,14))^2)`;
  - `embedded_2l`, subspace `[3,7]`, with the complementary numerator.
- Under `c1`, `(8,12,14)` also has `shared_1l` in subspace `[7]` and the same parent LMB.
- Under `c4`, `(7,8)` has the same `[7]`/`[3,7]` partition, but the complementary surface is `eset(2,4,10,13)`.
- Cuts `c2` and `c5` have no explicit association in the retained document.

The expressions use only the established `eta(star, eset(...))` syntax. Threshold subtraction remains enabled.

### Rejected exploratory changes

1. Replacing the duplicated `(7,8)` variants by a single shared `[7]` association failed generation. GammaLoop reported that cut group 1 variants `shared_1l` and `default` do not share disjoint coordinates in one parent LMB. Evidence: `guard/shared_1l_generation_failure.log`.
2. A temporary `1e-24` selector resolving the exact complementary-weight `0/0` allowed `q13` sampling, but the total behaved as `lambda^-3` at IR rank 3. All fits were extremely stable, yet the envelope `[3.0000744, 3.0015681]` lies in the campaign's marginal/inconclusive band rather than the pass region `p <= 2.75`. The regulator was rejected and removed. Evidence: `approaches/diagnostics/regularized_soft_q13.analysis.json`.
