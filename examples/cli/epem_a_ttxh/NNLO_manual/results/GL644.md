## Directive and precision diagnosis

Threshold subtraction is active in the raw evidence through `additional_contribution_sums.threshold_counterterm_0`. Nevertheless, disabling threshold subtraction on the exact `soft_e14` path changes its fitted envelope only from `2.956243..3.625732` to `2.956243..3.625732`; every displayed fit agrees at the shown precision. The exact correlated `e14` threshold-off envelope is likewise unchanged at `2.380627..3.920507`. Both threshold-on series are noisy and branch/window inconsistent rather than smooth failures.

Thus threshold-off does **not** materially restore the expected power. The required Arb gate is not met, no Arb run is attempted, and the generic f64 cancellation loss cannot support a threshold directive. No edit is accepted, so an edited-association threshold-only validation is not applicable. Threshold subtraction remains enabled in the final card and integration.
