In accordance with the one-prescription lease rule, no parent search or second construction was attempted; the provisional directive was immediately removed and the clean no-directive full-UV state regenerated.

The final graph is therefore valid but scientifically unresolved. Threshold subtraction stays enabled. Because no edit was accepted, threshold-only validation paths are not applicable; the correlated threshold-on paths above provide the relevant active-CT evidence.
