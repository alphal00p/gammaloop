## Remediation diagnosis

The continuation resolved the parent ambiguity from the earlier report. Six
spanning-tree parents reduce to three cut-relative embeddings; the
topology-selected common parent used in trials was `[3,7,9,13]`. Component
decomposition then showed that the original report had localized the aggregate
failure too narrowly:

- with only E33/E51 projected to `[7,9]`, those two components become safe at
  `p≈2`, but omitted/default E21 and E39 remain clean `p≈5` terms;
- projecting all four cut-2 associations to `[7,9]` cures q13 in Arb at
  `p=[1.999524,2.000043]`, but transfers clean failures to q14 (`p≈5`) and
  double soft (`p≈7`), so it was rejected;
- the mixed E21/E51=`[9]`, E33/E39=`[7,9]` trial passes q13 and q14 but still
  fails double soft at `p≈7`, entirely through E33/E39, so it was rejected;
- assigning all four to `[9]` gives excellent numerical behavior: q13 Arb
  `p=[1.999850,2.000012]`, q14 `p=[1.999998,2.000007]`, and double-soft Arb
  `p=[2.998821,3.000779]`, all with near-unit R2. However, its registry contains
  only cut-2 E33/E51; E21/E39 have algebraically disappeared and evaluator
  count drops from 13 to 11. This violates the requirement to retain proper
  subtraction for every assigned association and was rejected despite the
  attractive powers;
- the final minimal proper-subtraction trial projects only E33/E51 to `[9]`.
  It retains all 13 evaluators, but E21 and E39 defaults remain `p≈5` while
  E33/E51 are `p≈2`; the complete sum fails at
  `p=[4.999904,4.999999]`, minimum R2 `0.999999999949`.

The all-`[9]` trial's four IR-away threshold paths each had 100/100 positive
finite points, active total threshold subtraction, and essentially flat
`|p|<3.4e-6`. Those checks establish that E33/E51 subtraction itself remains
well behaved, but they cannot legitimize the missing cut-2 E21/E39 registry
entries. No singular complementary eta ratio was introduced: q13=q14=0 would
reproduce the known GL638 `0/0` common-zero defect.

Therefore no tested active-preserving projection cures every required region.
The final DOT intentionally contains no threshold directive rather than an
apparently convergent but subtraction-incomplete prescription.
