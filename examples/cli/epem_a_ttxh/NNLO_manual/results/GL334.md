## Routing and singular loci

GL334 has four loops, no `V_36`/`V_37`, and two gluons attached at distinct top vertices. The raw routing gives, spatially,

```text
e2  = K0-K2             H
e3  = K2                t
e4  = K0                t, lmb_id 0
e5  = K1                t, lmb_id 1
e6  = K0+K1-K2          t
e7  = K1                t
e9  = K2                t, lmb_id 2
e11 = -K1+K2+K3         t
e12 = K3                t, lmb_id 3
e13 = K2-K1             g
e14 = K1-K3             g
```

Therefore `q13=K2-K1` and `q14=K1-K3` are independent rank-3 soft constraints and their common locus has rank 6. No process-valid hard-collinear sector is present for this non-V36 topology.

At `q13=0`, E25/E35, E32/E57, E46*/E51*, and E59/E62/E65/E67 coalesce. At `q14=0`, the pairs are E59/E32, E62/E35, E65/E25, E67/E57, and E46*/E51*. At double soft, all eight nonstar active surfaces share one equation, while the starred E46/E51 equation is distinct and cannot vanish at the same radial scale.
