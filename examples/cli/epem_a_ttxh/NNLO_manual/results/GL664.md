## Topology, routing, cuts, and thresholds

GL664 is a four-loop graph with basis edges `(e3,e4,e5,e7)`. Its only massless internal edges are gluons e13/e14. The clean DOT retains exactly four `lmb_id` fields and contains no `pin`, `dir`, `lmb_rep`, or threshold-counterterm document.

Writing `(K0,K1,K2,K3)=(a,b,c,d)` and suppressing the zero incoming spatial sum, fresh conservation gives

```text
q2=b-a, q3=a, q4=b, q5=c, q6=b-a+c,
q7=q8=q10=d, q12=c+d-a, q13=a-c, q14=d-a.
```

Thus e13 soft is `c=a`, e14 soft is `d=a`, and the constraints are independent, with ranks 3, 3, and 6. Full unrestricted generation retained **144 orientations** and exactly two process-valid cuts:

|---:|---|---|
| 0 | `(2,6,7,13,14)` | none |
| 1 | `(2,4,7,14)` | right E51, E55, E47* |

The star marks the pinched E47 instance. The complete generated E-surface inventory is E25 `(7,8)`, E36 `(7,10)`, E44 `(2,3,6,7,12)`, E45 `(3,5,7,12)`, E47 `(4,6,7,12)`, E48 `(2,4,5,7,12)`, E51 `(7,12,13)`, E52 `(3,7,14)`, E53 `(2,4,7,14)`, E54 `(4,5,6,7,14)`, E55 `(5,7,13,14)`, and E56 `(2,6,7,13,14)`; only E47/E51/E55 are active in a process-valid cut.

With `Et(k)=sqrt(|k|^2+173^2)`, their equations are

```text
eta51 = Et(d)+Et(c+d-a)+|a-c|-1000
eta55 = Et(c)+Et(d)+|a-c|+|d-a|-1000
eta47 = Et(b)+Et(b-a+c)+Et(d)+Et(c+d-a)-1000.
```

At e13 soft all three equations are distinct. At e14 soft E51 and E55 coalesce to `Et(a)+Et(c)+|a-c|-1000`, while E47 remains distinct. At double soft E51/E55 coalesce to `2 Et(a)-1000`; E47 becomes `2 Et(a)+2 Et(b)-1000` and cannot vanish simultaneously. This exhausts the accessible soft/threshold families.
