## Routing and singular loci

GL368 has four loops, no `V_36` or `V_37`, and no applicable hard-collinear
sector. With the incoming spatial momentum sum equal to zero, the raw routing
is

```text
e2  = K1-K2                 H
e3  = K0+K2-K1              t
e4  = K0                    t, lmb_id 0
e5  = K1                    t, lmb_id 1
e6  = K2                    t
e7  = K1                    t
e9  = K2                    t, lmb_id 2
e11 = K0+K2+K3-K1           t
e12 = K0+K3-K1              g
e13 = K3                    g, lmb_id 3
e14 = K1-K3                 t
```

Thus `q12=K0-K1+K3` and `q13=K3` are independent rank-3 soft
constraints; double soft is `K3=0, K0=K1` with rank 6. At q12 soft,
E7/E52 coalesce. At q13 soft, E54/E55 coalesce. At double soft the three
distinct radial families are E7/E52, E54/E55, and starred E6/E17.

For `Et(k)=sqrt(|k|^2+173^2)`, the active distances used were


## Directive decision

No `threshold_counterterms` directive is accepted. The generic single-soft
limits and the evaluable q12 and starred-double intersections pass, while the
generic double limit is inconclusive, q13+E54/E55 is nonfinite, and two
double-soft threshold families are blocked before data production. Guessing a
projected embedding would violate the evidence requirement. Threshold
subtraction remains enabled. Because the DOT is unedited, no threshold-only
post-edit path is required.

## Conclusion

`threshold struct. edited? = no`; `converged? = yes` for the retained finite
checkpoint; `all limits OK? = inconclusive`. GL368 passes both independent
single-soft limits and the evaluable coalescing q12 and starred-double paths,
but the generic double-soft fit is inconclusive, q13+E54/E55 is nonfinite, and
both nonstar double-threshold families are blocked. The same CT validation
failure terminates integration after four completed iterations. No directive
can be justified without guessing the invalid projected embedding, so the DOT
remains unedited with threshold subtraction active and the unresolved evidence
is carried forward honestly.
