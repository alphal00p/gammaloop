## Routing and singular loci

GL592 has four loops and no `V_36` or `V_37`, hence no applicable
hard-collinear sector. With `a=K0`, `b=K1`, `c=K2`, `d=K3`, and zero
incoming spatial momentum sum, the raw routing is

```text
e2  = b-a             H
e3  = a               t, lmb_id 0
e4  = b               t, lmb_id 1
e5  = d               t
e6  = b+d-a           t
e7  = c               t, lmb_id 2
e8  = c               t
e10 = d               t, lmb_id 3
e11 = P0+P1           a, fixed spatial momentum 0
e12 = d-c             g = q12
e13 = d               t
e14 = a-d             g = q14
```

The q12 and q14 soft constraints are independent and have rank 3 each; their
common locus has rank 6. For `Et(k)=sqrt(|k|^2+173^2)`, the active equations
are

```text
eta5  = Et(a)+Et(d)+|a-d|-1000
eta6  = Et(a)+Et(c)+|d-c|+|a-d|-1000
eta27 = 2 Et(c)-1000
eta36 = Et(c)+Et(d)+|d-c|-1000.
```

At q12 soft, E5/E6 and E27/E36 coalesce. At q14 soft, E6/E36 coalesce,
while E5 and E27 remain separate. At double soft, all four active surfaces
coalesce at `2 Et(c)-1000`. Structurally coincident inactive E16/E17/E41/E42
were included in the target-distance series wherever unavoidable; no
unlisted generated threshold was silently approached.

## Directive decision

No `threshold_counterterms` directive is accepted. Both generic single-soft
limits and all five single-soft/threshold families pass. The generic and
correlated double-soft paths are formally inconclusive from poor fits and broad
p envelopes, so `all limits OK?` cannot be `yes`; however, they do not isolate
a threshold-driven failure or an unambiguous projected parent. Threshold
subtraction remains enabled. With no DOT edit, no post-edit threshold-only
validation is required.
