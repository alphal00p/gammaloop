# GL448 threshold/IR audit

## Outcome

- Final threshold structure edited: **no**. The task-start DOT and generation
  basis were restored byte-for-byte after the complete remediation search.
- Threshold structure investigated: **yes**. Projected one-/two-loop families,
  a topology-valid basis remap, and both complementary squared-eta polarities
  were generated and tested with threshold subtraction enabled.
- UV mode: **full local and integrated UV**; tailored final-state profile below.
- Process-valid cuts / unrestricted orientations: **6 / 512**.
- Collinear requirement: **none** (`V_36` and `V_37` are absent).
- All limits OK: **no**. The `q12` sector is an unresolved threshold-driven
  rank-three failure: the pristine counterterm aborts root validation, while
  every complete root-valid cure leaves a clean `p≈3` divergence.
- Integration diagnostic converged: **no**.

Every evaluable baseline `e13` or ordinary bridge sector either passes or is
formally inconclusive from cancellation-limited fit quality. The exact
threshold-off `q12` control passes at `p≈1`, so the repeated threshold-on abort
was treated as a counterterm-structure defect. The exhaustive complete-family
search below removes the abort but exposes the same stable nonintegrable
`p≈3` residue. No tested threshold-preserving construction cures it.

## Directive decision

Original and final compact specifications are both none. The pristine error is
cut group 2, left overlap group 0, projected E-surface instance 2. The generated
registry identifies that instance as E30 `(4,11,13)`, not E6 as the display
ordering initially suggested.

All 132 topology-valid generation bases were enumerated. The useful remap was
`(2,4,12,13)`, with new coordinates `(A,B,S,D)` and exact inverse

```text
(a,b,c,d) = (A,B,B+D-S,D).
```

Thus both soft gluons are explicit (`q12=S`, `q13=D`). All 17 stored approach
points and axes were transformed with this map and checked by exact numerical
reconstruction. The remap alone retains the E30 abort, so the basis was not used
to hide the failure and the final DOT restores `(2,4,7,13)`.

At `q12=0`, the complete cross-cut overlap comprises nine associations: E5 on
cuts 0, 2, 3, and 5; E30 and E36 on cut 2; E31 on cuts 0 and 3; and E47 on cut 5.
No partial-family result was accepted. The decisive ledger is:

| threshold-preserving candidate | exact generic `q12` result | decision |
|---|---|---|
| remapped basis, autogenerated CTs | same E30 center/radial-root abort | reject |
| all 20 associations projected to `[4]` | `p=2.99964..3.00017` | reject |
| isolated cut-2 E30 `[4,13]` or `[4]` | same E30 abort | reject |
| cut-2 E5/E30/E36 `[4]` family | same E30 abort; overlap is cross-cut | reject |
| complete nine-association `[4,13]` family | roots valid; `p=2.999865..3.000137` | reject |
| complete nine-association `[4]` family | roots valid; `p=2.999972..3.000033` | reject |
| complete `[4]`/`[4,13]` E5/E6 eta-squared partition | 36 evaluators; `p=2.999819..3.000227` | reject |
| same complete partition, swapped polarity | 36 evaluators; `p=2.999842..3.000190` | reject |

For the complete `[4,13]` trial, separate cut fits localize the residual:
cuts 0, 1, 4, and 5 scale approximately as `p=2,1,2,1`, whereas cuts 2 and 3
both scale as `p≈3`. This is why the investigation did not mistake the cured
root validator for a cured complete-graph limit.

For the two weighted trials, E5 `(7,13,14)` and E6 `(7,8)` are independent
away from the `q13` boundary and are valid E-surfaces on every affected cut.
The weights were `eta(E5)^2/(eta(E5)^2+eta(E6)^2)` and its exact complement in
the common `effective` frame. Both duplicated component registries are present
for every one of the nine associations. All 100 `q12` points are finite with
active counterterms, so the denominator is not exposed as a common zero on that
path. The candidate failed its first IR gate; consequently e13, double-soft, and
IR-away denominator certification was not promoted or claimed. An attempted
global E5/E30 pair was rejected structurally because E30 is not an E-surface on
every affected cut.

The complete `[4]` and `[4,13]` branch screens separately verify that both roots
and centers exist before interpreting either weighted sum. Their clean same-sign
rank-three residues, followed by both failed positive complementary polarities,
are the no-go evidence. No epsilon regulator, exact zero gate, disabled
subtraction, omitted cross-cut association, or timeout result is used.

## Conclusion

GL448 remains threshold-enabled and its final DOT is pristine. The remediation
turns the old root-validation-only diagnosis into a sharper unresolved result:
all complete root-valid one-loop, two-loop, and complementary constructions
expose a stable rank-three `q12` divergence, while threshold-off passes. The
tested directive space therefore contains no acceptable cure. Final
classification is **limits not OK / integration not converged**; the historical
finite first checkpoint does not override the IR failure.
