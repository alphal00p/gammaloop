# GL448 threshold/IR audit

## Outcome

- Threshold structure edited: **no**.
- UV mode: **full local and integrated UV**.
- Process-valid cuts / unrestricted orientations: **6 / 512**.
- Collinear requirement: **none** (`V_36` and `V_37` are absent).
- All limits OK: **inconclusive**.
- Integration diagnostic converged: **no**.

Every evaluable `e13` or ordinary bridge sector either passes or is formally inconclusive from poor fit quality. However, the generic `e12`, `e12` ordinary/three-particle correlations, and generic double-soft paths reproducibly abort threshold-counterterm center/radial-root validation, including with alternate hard geometry. Integration stops on the same error after its first 100-sample checkpoint. No smooth branch-consistent failure exists, so Arb and directive searches are not permitted.
