## Directive attempt and final state

The original and final graph use implicit default threshold CTs. A single GL297-style candidate was tried for the decisive `q12` bridge: force the cut-0 `E36/E42` and cut-1 `E31/E32/E36` associations into the common one-loop `subspace=[13]`, excluding soft edge 12. Clean generation rejected the first cut-1 association because `[13]` has eleven genuinely different compatible non-preferred parent LMB embeddings and explicitly required `parent_lmb`. No embedding was uniquely justified by the topology, so none was guessed. The candidate was immediately reverted, the original state and all baseline/threshold-off evidence were restored, and the rejection is preserved in `generate_projected_rejected.log` and its guard record.
