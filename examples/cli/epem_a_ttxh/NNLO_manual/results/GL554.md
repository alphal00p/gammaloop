## Directive diagnosis and rejected remedy

At q13=0, the affected pairs coalesce in their smallest common hard subspaces:

- cut 0: E5/E60 in `[3,10]` and E38/E66 in `[8,10]`;
- cut 3: E38/E66 in `[8,10]`;
- cut 5: E17/E50 in `[5,7]`.

A single minimal prescription projected both members of every pair onto those common edge sets, with no multiplier variants. Clean full-UV generation rejected the `[8,10]` association before producing a state: it has **ten genuinely different graph-global parent-LMB embeddings** and requires an explicit `parent_lmb`. The cut-compatible topology and available evidence do not select one uniquely. In accordance with the no-guess rule, no parent from the error list was chosen, no second prescription was attempted, and the entire provisional threshold document was removed. The failure and all candidate embeddings are preserved in `state_GL554/rejected_directive_generation.log`; the guard record is adjacent.

Because the directive could not be generated unambiguously, post-edit correlated and threshold-only checks are not applicable to a final edit. Threshold subtraction remains active, and the final DOT is the original clean graph rather than an unvalidated partial cure.
