- **Final graph:** accepted exact loop-momentum-basis change from edges `(4,5,6,11)` to `(4,6,11,14)`.
- **Threshold structure edited?:** no. The regenerated graph still has no explicit `threshold_counterterms` document; the evidence does not justify a speculative projected variant.

- **All limits OK?:** **yes**. All eight single-soft/correlated families pass,

- **Full UV generation:** yes, with local and integrated UV counterterms, 31 evaluators, and no compilation.
- **Mandatory all-orientation UV profile:** **pass for the selected final-state
  audit**. Generic random masks 4, 8, 12, and 15 cover q11, q14, their common

- **Collinear limits:** not applicable. The graph contains gluons `e11` and `e14` but no `V_36` or `V_37` vertex.

The accepted cure is deliberately a basis cure, not an unmotivated
counterterm projection. It makes both massless momenta direct generation
coordinates and removes the defective geometry that had made the nominal
generic `e14` probe sit on `q11=0` identically. With unrestricted cuts and
orientations, the two independent single-soft paths and the relevant
coalescing threshold families pass at `p≈2`. Rank-six Arb adjudication now
proves that all three physical double-soft totals also have `p≈2`; their
apparent f64 `p≈5..7` behavior is cancellation noise between individually
stable terms, not a threshold-counterterm divergence.

## Exact basis transform and topology

Let the original `(4,5,6,11)` coordinates be `(a,b,c,d)`. The final `(4,6,11,14)` coordinates are exactly

```text
(K0,K1,K2,K3) = (a,c,d,a-b-d),    b = K0-K2-K3.
```

No external spatial shift is involved. The important internal momenta become

```text
q4  = K0                 q5  = K0-K2-K3
q6  = K1                 q11 = K2
q14 = K3                 q3 = q9 = K1+K2+K3
q12 = K1+K3              q13 = K0-K3.
```

Thus `q11` and `q14` are independent direct coordinates, while every original approach point and axis has an exact inverse image. All 12 inherited path pairs were transformed with exact decimal arithmetic and checked by substitution against the original card. A new generic `q14` path was added with hard `q11`; the historical path is retained for provenance but excluded from the final suite because it enforces `q11=0` for every `lambda`.

## Double-soft precision adjudication

The f64 generic double-soft total is numerically misleading. Component localization finds:

- every individual original-cut contribution has stable rank-six scaling, with a global envelope `p=5.99989..6.00006` and `R²≈1`;
- every threshold-counterterm contribution scales near `p=2` (`p=1.99624..2.00048` in aggregate);
- only the cancellation-sensitive sum of original cuts, and therefore the total, has the poor f64 `p≈6..7` envelope.

The unrestricted Arb rerun evaluates all 100 signed points and gives:

| branch | 8 points | 12 points | 16 points |
|---|---|---|---|
| negative | `2.000001828 / 1.000000000` | `2.000002853 / 1.000000000` | `2.000004568 / 0.999999999999` |

Entries are `p/R²`. This is causal all-cut/all-orientation evidence: no `orientation_id`, discrete filter, or forced cut is present. It establishes a physical `p≈2` double-soft total and shows that float64 cancellation—not a missing threshold projection—caused the earlier apparent high power.

## Threshold-directive decision

Original compact specification: none. Final compact specification: none.

The resource strategy permits a projected or duplicated counterterm only when
a concrete coalescing family fails and the modified prescription restores the
cancellation while retaining threshold subtraction. Here the newly valid
generic `q14` path and every tested single-soft coalescing family pass, the
aggregate and cut-level CT sectors already have `p≈2`, and Arb restores all
three double-soft totals to `p≈2`. Adding guessed `[5]` or `[6]` projections
for E46/E73 or E58/E74 would therefore change a passing subtraction without
causal evidence. The exact basis change remains the minimal accepted cure.
