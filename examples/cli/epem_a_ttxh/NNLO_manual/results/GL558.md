## Routing and singular loci

GL558 has four loops and no `V_36` or `V_37`, hence no applicable
hard-collinear sector. With `a=K0`, `b=K1`, `c=K2`, and `d=K3`, and with
zero incoming spatial momentum sum, its raw diagnostic routing is

```text
e2  = b-a             H
e3  = a               t, lmb_id 0
e4  = b               t, lmb_id 1
e5  = b+c-a           t
e6  = c               t, lmb_id 2
e7  = d               t, lmb_id 3
e8  = d               t
e10 = b+c-a           t
e11 = P0+P1           a, fixed spatial momentum 0
e12 = b+c-a-d         g = q12
e13 = d-c             g = q13
e14 = a+d-c           t
```

The q12 and q13 soft constraints are independent and have rank 3 each; their
common locus has rank 6. For `Et(k)=sqrt(|k|^2+173^2)`, the active equations
are

```text
eta22 = Et(a)+Et(c)+Et(d)+Et(a+d-c)-1000
eta23 = Et(c)+Et(d)+|q13|-1000
eta24 = 2 Et(d)-1000
eta27 = Et(b)+Et(b+c-a)+Et(d)+Et(a+d-c)-1000
eta47 = Et(c)+Et(b+c-a)+|q12|+|q13|-1000
eta48 = Et(d)+Et(b+c-a)+|q12|-1000
eta69 = Et(b)+Et(d)+Et(b+c-a)+Et(a+d-c)-1000.
```

At q12 soft, E23/E47, E24/E48, and E27/E69 coalesce while E22 remains a
separate starred instance. At q13 soft, E23/E24, E47/E48, and E27/E69
coalesce while E22 remains separate. At double soft, nonstarred
E23/E24/E47/E48 share `2 Et(c)-1000`; starred E22/E27/E69 share
`2 Et(a)+2 Et(c)-1000`.

## Directive decision

No `threshold_counterterms` directive is accepted. Both generic single-soft
limits and all eight single-soft/threshold families pass. The generic and two
correlated double-soft paths remain formally inconclusive because of poor fits
and broad p envelopes, so `all limits OK?` cannot be `yes`. There is no
evidence-supported projected parent to edit. Threshold subtraction remains
enabled, and with no DOT edit no post-edit threshold-only validation is
required.
