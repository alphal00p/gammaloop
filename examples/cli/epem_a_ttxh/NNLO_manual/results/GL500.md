## Routing and singular loci

GL500 has four loops and no `V_36` or `V_37`, hence no applicable
hard-collinear sector. With zero incoming spatial momentum sum, the raw routing
is

```text
e2  = K1-K2             H
e3  = K0+K2-K1          t
e4  = K0                t, lmb_id 0
e5  = K2                t
e6  = K1                t, lmb_id 1
e7  = K2                t, lmb_id 2
e8  = K2                t
e11 = K3                t, lmb_id 3
e12 = K3-K2             g
e13 = K1+K3-K0-K2       g
e14 = K0+K2-K3          t
```

Thus q12 and q13 are independent rank-3 soft constraints, with rank 6 at
their common locus. For `Et(k)=sqrt(|k|^2+173^2)`, the active equations are

```text
eta40 = Et(K2)+Et(K3)+|q12|-1000
eta54 = Et(K0+K2-K1)+Et(K2)+|q12|+|q13|-1000
eta58 = Et(K0)+Et(K2)+Et(K3)+Et(K0+K2-K3)-1000.
```

At q13 soft E40 and E54 coalesce, while E58 remains a distinct starred
instance. At double soft E40/E54 share `2 Et(K2)-1000`; E58 remains
`2 Et(K0)+2 Et(K2)-1000`. At q12 soft none of the three active equations
coalesces generically, so all three were tested independently.
