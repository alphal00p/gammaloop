## Precision, threshold-off, and directive decision

For `e12+E35/E60`, exact threshold-off evaluation gives an inconclusive cancellation-noisy envelope `[1.336542,2.202473]` (all six fits below the (R^2) requirement), but materially removes the smooth (p\simeq4) behavior. For `e12+E62`, threshold-off is itself incoherent: envelope `[2.523976,9.082649]`, minimum (R^2=0.0311). It therefore does not meet the gate for Arb or a prescription search.

The smallest theoretically motivated `E35/E60` projection excludes the `q12` relative coordinate and keeps the common hard edges `[3,13]`. A guarded generation attempt applied it to cut-0/cut-2 `E35` and cut-5 `E60`, but GammaLoop found **19 genuinely distinct graph-global embeddings** already on the first association and required `parent_lmb`. The complete candidate list and error are preserved in `directive_generate.log`. Since the process-valid topology does not select a unique parent, the attempt was reverted; clean full-UV generation was repeated. Original and final specifications are both **none**, and post-edit threshold-only validation is not applicable.

## Second-pass threshold-CT remediation

The remediation pass did not stop at the ambiguous-parent error. Component-resolved replay explains why the apparently natural projections do not cure this graph. On the smooth `p~5` candidates, the leading residual at `lambda=-10^-6` is carried almost entirely by the cut-2 threshold counterterm; the cross-cut terms that should cancel it are parametrically tiny. Conversely, the only construction that returns the old `p~4` behavior does so after four evaluator contributions disappear. It is therefore not a valid threshold subtraction even though it generates and evaluates.

## Conclusion
