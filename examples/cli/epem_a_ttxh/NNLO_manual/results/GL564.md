## Precision, threshold-off, and directive decision

For `e12+E35/E60`, exact threshold-off evaluation gives an inconclusive cancellation-noisy envelope `[1.336542,2.202473]` (all six fits below the (R^2) requirement), but materially removes the smooth (p\simeq4) behavior. The single permitted Arb evaluation then independently reproduces the active result: envelope `[3.999828823,3.999950503]`, span `0.000121680`, all (R^2>0.9999999997), and target slopes within `1+-1.3e-5`.

For `e12+E62`, threshold-off is itself incoherent: envelope `[2.523976,9.082649]`, minimum (R^2=0.0311). It therefore does not meet the gate for Arb or a prescription search.

The smallest theoretically motivated `E35/E60` projection excludes the `q12` relative coordinate and keeps the common hard edges `[3,13]`. A guarded generation attempt applied it to cut-0/cut-2 `E35` and cut-5 `E60`, but GammaLoop found **19 genuinely distinct graph-global embeddings** already on the first association and required `parent_lmb`. The complete candidate list and error are preserved in `directive_generate.log`. Since the process-valid topology does not select a unique parent, the attempt was reverted; clean full-UV generation was repeated. Original and final specifications are both **none**, and post-edit threshold-only validation is not applicable.
