GL330 is a four-loop graph with basis edges `e4, e5, e6, e11`. Its two massless internal edges are gluons `e13` and `e14`; all remaining internal loop edges are massive tops or electroweak lines. The vertices are `V_141`, `V_134`, `V_137`, and `V_98`, so there is no triple- or four-gluon collinear sector. The clean DOT removes `pin`, `dir`, and `lmb_rep`, preserves all four `lmb_id` attributes, and contains no explicit `threshold_counterterms` document.

Writing `(a,b,c,d)=(K0,K1,K2,K3)` and using zero external spatial momentum, the raw diagnostic routing is

```text
q13 = a - b
q3 = q9 = a + c - b
q14 = a + c - b - d = q3 - d
q12 = d - a + b = d - q13
q11 = d
```

Thus the two soft constraints are independent and have ranks three and six for single- and double-soft limits, respectively.
