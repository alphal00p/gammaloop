preserves basis edges e3/e4/e6/e7 and contains none of `pin`, `dir`, `lmb_rep`,

Writing `(K0,K1,K2,K3)=(a,b,c,d)` and suppressing the incoming spatial sum,

```text

q12=c-d  (g), q14=a-c  (g).
```

Thus e12 soft is `d=c`, e14 soft is `c=a`, and independent double soft is
`d=c=a`; the corresponding IR ranks are 3, 3, and 6. With `Q=b+c-a`, the
active eta equations used for target validation are

```text

E40 = E47 = Et(c)+Et(d)+|c-d|-1000,
Et(k)=sqrt(|k|^2+173^2).
```

At e12 soft the coalescing families are E27/E40/E47 and E5/E6, with E10
standalone. At e14 soft they are standalone E5, E10, and E27 plus the
E6/E40/E47 family. At double soft E5/E6/E27/E40/E47 coalesce while E10 remains
the second independent threshold family; the final path activates both.
