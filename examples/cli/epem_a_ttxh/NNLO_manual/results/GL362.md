## Directive diagnosis

The attempted minimal prescription put the coalescing `E51=(6,11,12)` and `E61=(6,7,12,13)` counterterms for cut `(2,6,7)` in subspace `[6]`, and `E7=(5,9,12,13)` for cut `(2,5,9)` in subspace `[5]`. Generation rejected the first association because `[6]` admits **45 genuinely different graph-global embeddings** and explicitly required `parent_lmb`. The complete candidate list and original error are preserved in `generate_local_projected.log`.

The active cut topology does not select one parent uniquely. Per contract, no parent was guessed and no alternate prescription search was attempted. The DOT was restored to no explicit directive, regenerated cleanly, and all eleven threshold-on paths were rerun; the same `p=5` failure remains. Threshold subtraction stayed active in the final integration.

## Conclusion

GL362 has one confirmed all-orientation threshold-induced IR failure: `e13 + E7/E51/E61` scales as `lambda^-5` against rank three, including in Arb, while the exact threshold-off copy scales as `lambda^-1`. The obvious one-loop projection cannot be applied reproducibly without choosing among 45 parent embeddings. The graph is therefore left clean, threshold-enabled, and honestly marked **unresolved (`all limits OK = no`)**. Its numerical checkpoint is converged only as a **local-UV-only/incomplete** diagnostic.
