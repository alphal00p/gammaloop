GL654 is a four-loop graph with canonical LMB basis edges `(4,5,6,7)`. Its massless gluons are `e13` and `e14`; it has no `V_36` or `V_37`, so no hard-collinear sector is required. With zero incoming spatial momentum,

```text
q13 = K0 - K3
q14 = -K0 + K1 - K2 + K3
q3  = K0 - K1 + K2
q12 = -K0 + K1 + K3.
```

One soft constraint has rank 3 and the independent pair has rank 6. Double soft imposes `K0=K3` and `K1=K2`.

## Directive diagnosis

Threshold-counterterm contributions are present (though tiny) on the generic and correlated single-soft evidence. On the chosen symmetric common double-soft path the threshold counterterm is exactly zero, so the threshold-on and threshold-off series coincide. This does not reveal an IR failure: the complete threshold-on series already passes at rank 6.

Consequently there is no basis for changing associations or introducing a partition. In particular, the GL638 complementary-partition construction and its rejected exact `0/0` regulator are neither needed nor copied. Because the DOT is unedited, an edited-association threshold-only verification is not applicable. The generated threshold subtraction remains enabled for integration.
