## Topology and fresh routing

GL310 has four loops, two internal gluons e11/e14, two separate quark-gluon exchange pairs, and no `V_36` or `V_37`; hence there is no mandatory hard gluon-collinear sector. The clean DOT preserves four `lmb_id` fields on e4/e5/e9/e12 and contains none of `pin`, `dir`, `lmb_rep`, or `threshold_counterterms`.

With spatial incoming sum zero, the raw routing is

```text

q4  = K0                 (t, lmb_id 0)
q5  = K1                 (t, lmb_id 1)
```

Thus e11 and e14 provide two independent rank-three soft constraints, with a rank-six common limit. No other massless internal edge exists. At e11 soft, E26/E31/E36/E48 merge on the same two-top equation and E58/E61 merge on a four-top equation. At e14 soft, E26/E48 and E58/E61 merge. At double soft, E26/E31/E36/E48 merge while E58/E61 remain their second threshold family.

## Threshold-directive decision

No `threshold_counterterms` directive was added. Threshold subtraction remained active throughout; correlated JSON shows nonzero threshold-counterterm contributions (for example, the first e14/E26-E48 point has original `4.8971e-40` and CT `2.9727e-39-6.2321e-41 i`). The clean e14 cancellation passes, while the e11/double evidence is precision-inconclusive rather than a threshold-proven failure. Guessing a parent projection would therefore violate the diagnosis gate. No threshold-only path is required for this unedited graph.
