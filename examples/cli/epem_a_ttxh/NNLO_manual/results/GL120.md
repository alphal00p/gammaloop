## Conclusion

GL120 receives no threshold directive. All required rank-three limits pass across the complete unrestricted orientation sum. The generic and both active-threshold double-soft tests remain cancellation-noise inconclusive and are unchanged with threshold subtraction disabled, so they do not provide the smooth threshold-driven failure required for Arb or a scientific edit. The guarded integration is finite with zero nonfinite/unstable samples but does not converge by the campaign's absolute-error criterion because `|re|` has 83.38% relative error. Max replay identifies two hard threshold loci and one already-covered `e13` soft locus, without evidence supporting a new directive.
