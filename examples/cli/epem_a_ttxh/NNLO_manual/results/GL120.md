# GL120 manual IR-safe threshold audit

## Outcome

- **Threshold structure edited:** no. Every single-soft and correlated
  single-soft limit passes with threshold subtraction enabled. Exact
  threshold-off copies of the three f64-noisy double-soft paths are
  byte-equivalent, and identical-geometry Arb reruns resolve all three totals;
  there is no threshold-driven failure that would justify a directive.
- **All limits OK:** **yes**. The eight rank-three families pass directly, and
  the generic plus both correlated rank-six families pass in Arb at `p≈2`.
- **UV mode:** full local and integrated UV. Fresh generation completed with
  twelve evaluators; no local-only fallback was used.
- **Mandatory UV audit:** **pass** for physical masks 6, 10, 14, and 15 on
  all 264 orientations and their summed records.
- **Integration converged:** **yes**. The fresh 20,800-sample checkpoint has
  componentwise-absolute relative errors of 19.47% and 20.46%, with zero
  nonfinite evaluations.
- **Precision decision:** reduced Arb was used only for the three existing
  nondegenerate double-soft geometries. Each produced 32/32 finite points and
  passed the complete 8/12/16-point hierarchy, so no broader escalation was
  needed.
