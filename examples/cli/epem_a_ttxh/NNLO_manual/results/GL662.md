## Topology, routing, cuts, and thresholds

GL662 is a four-loop graph with basis edges `(e3,e4,e5,e7)`. Its only massless internal edges are gluons e13/e14, and it contains neither `V_36` nor `V_37`, so no hard-collinear family is required. The clean DOT has exactly four `lmb_id` fields and no `pin`, `dir`, `lmb_rep`, or threshold-counterterm document.

Writing `(K0,K1,K2,K3)=(a,b,c,d)` and suppressing the vanishing incoming spatial sum, momentum conservation gives

```text
q2=b-a, q3=a, q4=b, q5=c, q6=b-a+c,
q7=q8=q10=d, q12=a, q13=d-a, q14=a-c.
```

Thus e13 soft is `d=a`, e14 soft is `c=a`, and the two constraints are independent, with ranks 3, 3, and 6. Full unrestricted local-only generation retained **128 orientations**, no forced cut/orientation filter, and two process-valid cuts:

| cut | edges | active association |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | `(2,6,7,13,14)` | none |
| 1 | `(2,4,7,13)` | right E39=`(5,7,13,14)` |

With `Et(k)=sqrt(|k|^2+173^2)`, the only active threshold equation is

```text
eta39 = Et(c)+Et(d)+|d-a|+|a-c|-1000.
```

This exhausts the process-valid soft/threshold inventory.
