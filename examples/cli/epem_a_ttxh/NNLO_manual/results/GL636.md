# GL636 manual IR-safe threshold audit

## Outcome

- **Threshold structure edited:** no. A candidate shared `[7]` projection for the coalescing `(7,8)` / `(3,7,13)` family was generated and tested, but rejected and removed from the final DOT.
- **All limits OK:** **inconclusive**. The two generic single-soft paths and both `e14` correlated paths pass. The generic and correlated double-soft paths and two `e13` correlated paths remain fit-quality/envelope inconclusives.
- **UV mode:** full local and integrated UV.
- **Integration converged under the campaign's rough rule:** no. The checkpoint is finite with no nonfinite samples, but the componentwise-absolute imaginary relative error is `77.1624%`, just above the `75%` cutoff.
- **Scientific conclusion:** the baseline exposes a threshold-driven `e13` problem, but the smallest tested common projection is not a cure: it changes the target to a stable marginal `p≈3` and simultaneously turns three previously passing `e14` sectors into stable marginal behavior. The autogenerated threshold structure is therefore retained pending a valid joint construction.

## Topology and process-valid geometry

- Four-loop basis edges: `(3,4,7,10)`. Momentum conservation gives `e3=K0`, `e4=K1`, `e7=e8=K2`, `e10=e5=K3`, `q13=K2-K0`, `q14=K0-K3`, and `q12=K2-K0+K3`.
- The only massless QCD edges are gluons `e13` and `e14`. There is no `V_36` or `V_37` vertex, so no hard gluon-collinear family applies.
- Full structure display reports **392 orientations** and six cuts: `(2,6,12,13)`, `(2,6,10)`, `(2,6,7,13,14)`, `(2,4,12,13,14)`, `(2,4,10,14)`, and `(2,4,7,13)`.
- Relevant E-surfaces are E10=`(3,10,14)`, E16=`(5,10)`, E29=`(8,10,13,14)`, E30=`(8,12,14)`, E31=`(7,8)`, E37=`(4,6,8,12)`, E50=`(3,7,10,12)`, E55=`(3,12,13,14)`, E56=`(3,7,13)`, E59=`(5,12,13)`, and E69=`(5,7,13,14)`.
- No orientation filter, `force_cuts`, or selected-orientation shortcut appears in the card. All reported paths use the complete generated orientation/cut sum.

## Tested threshold modification and rejection

At `e13 -> 0`, E31=`(7,8)` and E56=`(3,7,13)` coalesce in cuts 0 and 1. Following the GL297/GL638 projected-subspace strategy, both were temporarily assigned a shared `[7]` projection.

Parent-LMB resolution was itself informative:

1. Explicit parent `[3,4,7,10]` failed because raised sibling E37=`(4,6,8,12)` has no radial dependence in that common parent.
2. Inferred parent selection failed with different resolved parents 36 and 23.
3. Explicit parent `[6,7,12,13]` (display LMB 23) failed with parents 54 and 23.
4. Explicit parent `[3,6,7,10]` (display LMB 54) supports the raised siblings and completed full-UV generation: 25 evaluators, `41.930442652 s`, zero compile time.

The successful-parent trial still fails the scientific acceptance test:

Threshold-only E31 and E56 scans are finite and essentially flat (`|p| < 8.6e-6` and `|p| < 3.1e-7`); their formal labels are inconclusive only because fitting constant floating-point data gives low `R²`. They do not rescue the newly created marginal IR powers. The directive was therefore removed rather than transferring the divergence into previously healthy sectors. Failed-parent logs and the successful trial state are preserved below `state_GL636/projected*`.
