# GL636 manual IR-safe threshold audit

## Outcome

- **Threshold structure edited:** no. Four increasingly structured cures were generated and tested, including the requested shared-one-loop / genuine-two-loop partition. Every candidate was rejected and removed from the final DOT because it either spoiled previously safe limits or produced an invalid projected radial root.
- **All limits OK:** **inconclusive**. The two generic single-soft paths and both `e14` correlated paths pass. The generic and correlated double-soft paths and two `e13` correlated paths remain fit-quality/envelope inconclusives.
- **UV mode:** full local and integrated UV generation. The mandatory adaptive per-orientation profile exhausted its guard without writing a JSON, so UV certification is inconclusive.
- **Integration converged under the campaign's rough rule:** no. The checkpoint is finite with no nonfinite samples, but the componentwise-absolute imaginary relative error is `77.1624%`, just above the `75%` cutoff.
- **Scientific conclusion:** the baseline exposes a threshold-driven `e13` problem. The ttH-deformation-inspired partition localizes the tradeoff sharply, but all tested common-parent realizations either transfer it to stable marginal `p≈3` behavior in healthy sectors or become radial-invalid. The autogenerated threshold structure is therefore retained pending a construction that treats the complete E10/E29/E30/E31/E56 coalescence web jointly.

Parent-LMB resolution was itself informative:

### Shared-one-loop / genuine-two-loop partition

The next trial implemented the `ttH_defo.pdf` prescription directly. In cuts 0 and 1, E31 received a shared one-loop `[7]` term and a genuine two-loop `[3,7]` term, E56 used the same shared `[7]` direction, and all terms used common parent `[3,6,7,10]`. The E31 pieces were weighted by the complementary smooth partition
`eta(E31)^2/(eta(E31)^2+eta(E56)^2)` and
`eta(E56)^2/(eta(E31)^2+eta(E56)^2)`, so their sum retains the original threshold subtraction away from the coalescence.

### Broader common-parent probes

Projecting all active cut-0/cut-1 associations onto `[3]` in common parent `[3,4,13,14]` tested whether a larger shared family was required. It generated with 25 evaluators, but generic `e13` immediately became `p=3.000003..3.000081`; the next E50 path failed overlap-center validation. This was rejected and preserved in `remediation_shared_soft_parent`.

The final bounded trial used topology-valid parent `[6,10,13,14]`. With parent coordinates
`P6=-K0+K1+K3`, `P10=K3`, `P13=K2-K0`, and `P14=K0-K3`, the inverse is
`K0=P10+P14`, `K1=P6+P14`, `K2=P10+P13+P14`, `K3=P10`.
Thus the autogenerated genuine plane `delta K=(a,a,b,0)` is exactly `[13,14]`, while shared `[10]` gives `delta K=(t,0,t,t)` and fixes both unresolved momenta `q13` and `q14`. All seven active cut-1 surfaces were rebased into that single parent; E31 was partitioned between shared `[10]` and genuine `[13,14]`, E56 used shared `[10]`, and the other surfaces retained the exact rebased `[13,14]` plane.

This candidate completed full generation with 26 evaluators (`42.330051561 s`, `255,115,264` bytes internal peak RAM). Generic `e13` remained a clean pass, `p=1.992525..2.000090`, confirming the coordinate transform itself. Generic `e14`, however, aborted at cut group 1 left projected E-surface instance 5 after the final f64 stability level because its center/radial root was invalid. A radial-invalid counterterm cannot retain threshold subtraction, so the candidate was rejected without interpreting missing downstream fits. Its DOT SHA-256 while staged was `1d8c6e905be72c5f39a98aac3415adfb02501944e04d914691d6a29b340362b1`; the generated state, completed fit, and guard logs are preserved in `remediation_c1_common_parent13_partition` and `remediation_guards`.

The remaining design space is narrower than at the start. The coupled limits form one five-surface web: under `e13` soft, E31 coalesces with E56 and E10 with E29/E30; under `e14` soft, E31 coalesces with E30 and E56 with E29. Treating only E31/E56 repairs one cancellation while moving the defect to the others. A future cure should therefore use one partition of unity over the entire E10/E29/E30/E31/E56 web, with a common parent whose shared one-loop direction has a valid radial root for every raised sibling, rather than another isolated two-surface projection.
