## Topology and complete threshold inventory

The generation basis is `(e3,e4,e7,e10)`. Writing its spatial loop variables as `(a,b,c,d)`,

```text
q12 = a-b+c-d
q14 = b-c
w   = -a+b+d
```

The only massless internal edges are gluons e12 and e14. There is no `V_36` or `V_37`, so no hard-collinear family is required. The process has eight valid cuts, 516 unrestricted orientations, and the seven active surfaces E14, E30, E47, E52, E64, E67, and E70:

## Accepted threshold prescription

At q12 soft, E30/E52 coalesce with E64/E70. The parent `[3,4,7,12]` makes edge 12 the soft coordinate while direction `[7]` moves their shared massive momentum at fixed q12. The final DOT therefore projects these seven associations onto `[7]`:

- E64 on cuts 2, 3, and 5;
- E70 on cuts 4 and 5;
- E30 and E52 on cut 5.

E30 and E52 additionally receive a genuine `[3,7]` variant. For either target surface, define in the runtime `effective` frame

```text
s12^2 = Q3(12,1)^2 + Q3(12,2)^2 + Q3(12,3)^2
D     = MT^2 s12^2 + eta(target)^4
w_[7]   = MT^2 s12^2 / D
w_[3,7] = eta(target)^4 / D.
```

The weights are complementary wherever `D != 0`; no exact gate, epsilon regulator, or disabled subtraction is used. There is a formal common-zero `0/0` at exactly `q12=eta(target)=0`. All correlated tests skip their midpoint, so a final integration remains necessary to check that generated root/effective-frame evaluation never exposes that locus.

The physical runtime interpretation must use the serialized CT-root frame, not the names of the two branches. At `|lambda|=10^-6` on q12+E67, E30/E52 have `w_[7]≈2.8292e-14` and `w_[3,7]≈0.999999999999972`; the unsafe bare `[7]` term is suppressed from about `2.598e-13` to `7.35e-27`. The `[3,7]` branch also dominates the native q12+E30/E52 approach (`w_[7]≈6.62e-13`) and supplies its safe cancellation. It would be incorrect to describe the `[7]` branch as the native-threshold region merely from the algebraic labels.

## IR-away threshold-only checks

E30, E52, and E70 each have 100 finite records and active CTs. Their totals are flat through the target threshold, with `|p| <= 5.104e-6`, `|p| <= 1.103e-6`, and `|p| <= 5.394e-5`, respectively. The generic analyzer labels these constant plateaus inconclusive because fitting numerical jitter gives low `R^2`; for the threshold-only rank-zero criterion `|p| <= 0.25`, all three pass.

The complementary weights are nontrivial on these loci: E30 serializes approximately `0.004822/0.995178`, while E52 serializes approximately `0.650674/0.349326`. Threshold subtraction is therefore retained rather than silently gated away.

The first E64 geometry reached an unrelated right-side E14 overlap-center failure before it could test E64. The card therefore stages:

```text

E70: (a;b;c;d)=(800,0,L; 0,0,L; 0,0,100; 800,0,0), vary d

```

Both alternatives analytically place the requested target on shell while keeping E14 absent. E70 completes with the flat envelope `-5.39304e-5..5.39367e-5` and minimum `R^2=0.877821`; its low fit quality is the expected numerical jitter of a constant plateau. E64 still emits invalid-radial refusals and aborts before JSON serialization:

