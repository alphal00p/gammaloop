Expand description

Selects and evaluates tensor pairs inside one ready product operation.

The execution strategy owns graph scheduling. A contraction strategy sees the current ready operation and mutable value store, and returns the leaf that replaces that operation. Implementations may support incremental graph rewriting, but they do not own a persistent plan.
