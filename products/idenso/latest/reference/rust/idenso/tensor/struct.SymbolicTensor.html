Expand description

A fully symbolic tensor, with no concrete values.

This tensor is used to represent the structure of a tensor, and is used to perform symbolic contraction. Currently contraction is just a multiplication of the atoms, but in the future this will ensure that internal indices are independent accross the contraction.

It can also serve as a tensor structure while retaining the symbolic expression and the ordered external-index structure across transformations.
