## (e) How the symbolic forms were obtained and checked

- **Closed forms.** 1L-3L and the poles of 4L: closed forms from the literature and the MATAD/FMFT expansions (harmonic polylogarithms at the sixth root of unity, the MATAD constants), carried exactly by vakint. 4L finite parts: exact in 50 lifted four-loop constants (Bessel moments, two-dimensional ε-jets) computed to 2348-20000 digits (S*). Some S rows rest on numerical identities of the constant basis checked to thousands of digits (8 identities; PRECISION-TABLES.md).
- **Five loops.** Structural zeros from the UV forest bound and exact rows from pole cancellation; 196 of the 591 exact 5L orders are PSLQ identifications (9 S*) and 4 measured zeros, each passing the 40-excess-digit bar (numerical identifications, not proofs). The other 5L orders are numerical (high-precision grids and dispersive gluing; digits = validated significant digits).
- **R operation (preview).** Pole coefficient K G = (A) − Δ: part (A) = −K Σ_S Δ(S) G/S from the subdivergences (ordinary massive R operation, exact, lower-loop coefficients of vakint); Δ = overall counterterm, a pure number: absent for ω < 0 (complete result), otherwise fitted as an integer relation of [numeric − (A)] in the basis 1, ζ3, π⁴, ζ5, π⁶, ζ3², ζ7, ζ3π⁴ (accepted at a margin ≥ 20 digits; to be confirmed by R*).
- **Numerical check.** Every symbolic form is compared with the numerical value it replaces, to that value’s full claimed digits (user ruling 2026-11-10), at adoption and again when the release tooling integrates it; a mismatch blocks the substitution. Pending entries list their digits of agreement; where they fall short of the claimed digits the entry is flagged.
